# 42Maru

> 42Maru is a South Korean AI deep-tech company building a Deep Semantic TA (Text Analytics) and QA (Question Answering) platform that understands the true intent of a user's question and returns a single answer from large unstructured document sets. Its cognitive-search stack combines document retrieval with Machine Reading Comprehension (MRC), and is delivered as enterprise chatbots, question-answering engines, and knowledge-management search. 42Maru ranked #1 on Stanford's SQuAD 2.0 machine-reading benchmark and is a Techstars London alumnus.
